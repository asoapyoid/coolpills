# Cool Pills Wiki

Cool Pills is a browser extension that helps you send videos from supported websites to [Coolhole](https://coolhole.org). It adds CH and CP controls to detected videos, a Q+ waiting list, playback history, and automatic recovery for some links Coolhole cannot play.

The current release is **v3.1.7**. See the [release notes and browser ZIPs](https://github.com/asoapyoid/coolpills/releases/tag/v3.1.7).

## What the buttons do

- **CH** sends a detected video to Coolhole. When possible, its title, duration, and thumbnail are included.
- **UN** removes a video that is already in the room queue.
- **CP** goes Fishing by default, automatically hooking when a fish bites. You can change its action to add to Q+, schedule, copy the video link, open or focus Coolhole, do nothing, or run Custom (experimental).
- **Q+** holds items locally until Coolhole has room, or adds them when auto-queue is enabled.

Set **Max Queued** to 0 for no personal queue cap; Coolhole's room limit still applies.

Prefer the original userscript? [Run Cool Pills with Tampermonkey from Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/591925-cool-pills-youtube-coolhole-queue-buttons).
